        Step 4 of 8: How long is the walk?

        The board uses these to say when to leave. Change any that are wrong; leave one empty for none.

        Step 5 of 8: Keys

        Train and bus times come from free services that give you a key. Keys are never shown here: paste one to save it, or leave a box empty to keep the key you have.

        Step 6 of 8: Which views?

        Show the departures on

        Each view shows the departures you picked; with a station and stops, the next step asks which each shows. Tick one or both; you can change this later on the settings site.

        Step 7 of 8: What to show

        With a station and stops, pick which departures each view shows. The departures' times always show: pick how much else each view shows; to choose item by item, use What to show on its settings page, now or later.

        Step 8 of 8: Check and save

        Only this changes. Everything else stays as it is.

        Saving first looks up which bus companies serve your stops and builds their timetable. That can take a few minutes; you can leave this page open while it runs.

        The timetable is built. The roads for the bus maps are still being found, for up to 5 minutes; until then the maps draw straight lines between stops. Wait for them, or carry on and they finish on their own.
